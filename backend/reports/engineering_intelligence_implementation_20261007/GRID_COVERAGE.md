# Grid coverage

Status on every stored grid label: `candidate`.

The rule is a whole line that is one or two letters, or a one- or two-digit number, on a foundation, framing, or roof plan, outside the title block. The list is capped at 80 per set. That cap was hit on all four projects, so the list is a sample of candidates, not a complete bubble survey.

Rejected as grids:

- `4'-6"`, `10'-0"`, `14'-8"`, `+14'-6"`, `-0'-6"` are dimensions
- `W/ SPEC` and `T/ SLAB` are not grids and not sheets
- A bare number is not promoted from “candidate label” to a grid id

No grid is attached to a member.
