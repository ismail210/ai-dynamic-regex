# Occurrence coverage

An occurrence is a whole-word copy of a schedule mark on a sheet other than the sheet that defines it. `quantity` is null. The count is a text-hit count.

| Project | defined_and_seen | defined_but_not_seen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Burrville | 15 | 1 |
| OSSE | 26 | 1 |
| Struct.pdf | 46 | 4 |
| Brandywine | 4 | 0 |

Grid and level on each hit stay empty. A mark that is not seen is not a quantity of zero. Marks that exist only as section text (`W12X40` with no schedule mark) are not added to this ledger.
